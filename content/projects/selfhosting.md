Most of what I work with on a day-to-day basis is self-hosted, either on my own hardware or colocated.

All of my infrastructure is hosted on Debian-based systems (yes, even the routers!) and the majority
of it is high-availability, with redundant physical servers and core routing.

You can check on the status of my services at [status.zirco.dev](https://status.zirco.dev).
